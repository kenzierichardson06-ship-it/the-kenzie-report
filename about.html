About The Kenzie Report

The Kenzie Report is an independent political commentary publication focused on British politics, current affairs, analysis and open debate.

What the site does

The publication brings together full articles, breaking updates, reader polls, debates and reader discussion. X is used as a faster-moving social feed; the website is the home for the full publication archive.

Commentary and reporting

Opinion and analysis are presented as such. Factual claims are intended to be checked and attributed where appropriate, and readers can report comments that breach the discussion rules.
