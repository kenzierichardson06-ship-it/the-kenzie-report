About the brand

The Kenzie Report

The Kenzie Report is a political commentary publication focused on British politics, current affairs and open debate.

The website is the home for full articles, breaking updates, polls and debates, while X provides a faster-moving social feed.

How the site works

Commentary & analysis

Opinion and analysis should be presented as such. Factual claims should be checked and attributed where appropriate.

Corrections

If an article contains an error, corrections can be made through the CMS and the published story can be updated.

Independence

The Kenzie Report is an independent publication and is not presented as an official government, party or campaign website.
